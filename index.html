Day 2Friday · June 12
Mt. Fuji 🗻 · Private Van · Pre-Dawn
5 AM departure · Kawaguchiko sunrise · Chureito Pagoda · Oshino Hakkai
Mt. Fuji Day
Private van booked · pick-up at hotel lobby 5:00 AMDriver meets at Shinjuku hotel lobby. ~90 min door-to-door to Lake Kawaguchi. Kids sleep in the van on the way. Book via Klook, Viator or GetYourGuide — search "Mt Fuji private tour 8 passengers Shinjuku". Budget ~¥35,000–55,000 total for the van (split across 2 families). Include Chureito Pagoda, Kawaguchiko, Oshino Hakkai and optional 5th Station in the brief. Book 4–6 weeks ahead.
Why Friday June 12 is the perfect dayWeekday = fewest visitors at all Fuji viewpoints. June mornings are clearest before 9 AM — leaving at 5 AM puts you at the lake by 6:30 AM in the golden window. By noon clouds typically build, so the early start is everything. Manraag can nap in the van both ways.
4:30 AM
Wake up · light breakfast at hotel
Keep it quick — grab onigiri and drinks from the hotel vending machine or the 7-Eleven downstairs. Keep Manraag in warm layers; Fuji area is ~16–18°C at dawn even in June.
Food
5:00 AM
🚐 Private van departs Shinjuku hotel
Driver picks up at lobby. Kids settle in and sleep. Highway is empty at this hour — smooth, fast drive southwest through the dark towards Yamanashi. Pre-dawn Tokyo glittering from the highway is a sight in itself.
Kids sleep in van
6:30 AM
Lake Kawaguchi · Fuji sunrise 🌅
Arrive as the sky shifts from navy to gold. Mt. Fuji — perfectly symmetrical, snow-capped — reflected in the mirror-still lake. Completely empty at this hour. This is the moment that makes the 5 AM wake-up entirely worth it. Manraag waking up to see Fuji for the first time is a memory that lasts forever.
Must-do momentFamily-friendly
7:15 AM
Chureito Pagoda · the iconic Japan photograph 🏯
The 5-storey red pagoda with Fuji perfectly framed behind it — the most famous photograph in Japan. 398 steps up from Shimoyoshida (15 min by van from the lake). Manraag will need carrying up, but every step is worth it. Best light is 7–8 AM. Arrive before any tour buses for the shot of a lifetime.
Most iconic Fuji photo
9:00 AM
Breakfast · lakeside café with Fuji view ☕
Several cafes open by 9 AM along the Kawaguchi lake shore. Eat with Mt. Fuji right in front of you. Some have terrace seating on the water — extraordinary setting for a family breakfast. Well deserved after the early start.
Food
10:00 AM
Oshino Hakkai · 8 sacred spring pools 💧
8 crystal-clear pools fed by ancient Mt. Fuji snowmelt — water so clear you can see every stone on the bottom. Traditional thatched village setting. Giant koi fish that Manraag will obsess over. Easy flat walk, completely stroller-friendly. One of the most beautiful spots around Fuji and rarely crowded. 20 min from the lake by van.
Perfect for ManraagMust-do
11:30 AM
Fuji Subaru Line 5th Station (optional) ⛰️
If the sky is still clear, ask the driver to head to the 5th Station (2,305m altitude — halfway up the mountain). Stand on Fuji itself, breathe the thin mountain air, see the volcanic rock and often snow. Spectacular. Takes ~1 hr round trip from Kawaguchiko. Skip if clouds have moved in — not worth the journey if visibility is low.
Weather permittingFamily-friendly

Hoto is thick flat noodle hot pot unique to the Mt. Fuji / Yamanashi region — rich miso broth, kabocha pumpkin, root vegetables. Warming and deeply satisfying. Try Houtou Fudou near Kawaguchiko — famous for serving in iron pots shaped like Mt. Fuji itself.
Regional specialtyMust-try

Family-friendly

Harajuku · Shibuya · teamLab Planets 🎨
Fashion street · world's busiest crossing · immersive digital art
Tokyo
Book teamLab ahead: teamLab Planets tickets must be pre-booked online — morning slots sell out weeks in advance. Book the 8:30 AM entry on klook.com or teamlab.art. Pack a spare set of clothes for Manraag — the water rooms guarantee getting wet!

teamLab Planets TOKYO · Toyosu 🎨
Stunning immersive digital art museum — walk barefoot through water rooms, drift through floating flower gardens, lose yourself in mirror infinity rooms. Morning slot is least crowded and most magical. Manraag will be completely awestruck. All ages are mesmerised.
Must-doFamily-friendly

Food
1:00 PM
Harajuku · Takeshita Street 🛍️
Tokyo's most colourful fashion street — crepes with every topping imaginable, candy floss, cosplay outfits, quirky accessories and K-pop merchandise. The teens will want hours here. Budget ¥2,000–3,000 each for snacks and trinkets. About 30 min by subway from Toyosu.
Must-doShoppingFamily-friendly
2:30 PM
Meiji Jingu Shrine · forested calm
Peaceful forested Shinto shrine — 5-min walk from Takeshita Street. Beautiful contrast to the Harajuku chaos. Massive torii gate entrance, gravel paths through giant cypress trees. Good reset for Manraag before the Shibuya energy.
Culture
3:30 PM
Shibuya Crossing 🚦
The world's busiest pedestrian intersection — 3,000 people crossing from all directions simultaneously when the lights change. Watch from Starbucks 2F or Mag's Park rooftop above for the bird's-eye view, then go down and cross it yourself. Thrilling for absolutely everyone. 10-min walk from Harajuku.
Must-doFamily-friendly
4:30 PM
Shibuya shopping · 109 · Hikarie · Loft
Shibuya 109 — 8 floors of Japanese youth fashion (teens love this). Hikarie — lifestyle and design goods. Loft — stationery, lifestyle, Japanese gifts. All within a 5-min walk of the crossing.
Shopping
6:00 PM
Asakusa · Senso-ji Temple at dusk 🏮
Tokyo's oldest temple at dusk is magical — the giant red lantern glows, crowds thin, the mood is serene. Walk Nakamise Shopping Street for traditional snacks and souvenirs. About 30 min by subway from Shibuya. Try the famous ningyo-yaki fish-shaped cakes.
Must-doFamily-friendly
7:30 PM
Dinner · Asakusa · tempura or yakitori

Inter-City Travel Plan
All legs · Shinkansen · JR Pass recommendation
All four inter-city legs are covered by Shinkansen (bullet train) — fast, comfortable and perfect for families with kids and luggage. A 7-day JR Pass activated on Day 5 (June 15) covers all four Shinkansen legs and pays for itself on the Tokyo–Kyoto leg alone.
💡 JR Pass Recommendation A 7-day JR Pass (activated June 15, valid through June 21 — covers Tokyo→Kyoto, Kyoto→Tokyo, and N'EX departure) covers: Tokyo→Kyoto, Kyoto→Osaka, Osaka→Tokyo, and the N'EX airport express. Cost: ~¥50,000/adult, ~¥25,000/child. The Tokyo→Kyoto Shinkansen alone costs ~¥14,000 one-way. With 4 adults + 2 teens, the pass pays for itself on Day 1 of activation. Buy online before leaving India at jrpass.com — cannot be bought in Japan.
🗼 Tokyo → ⛩️ Kyoto
Day 5 morning · June 15
LEG 1
🚄 Nozomi Shinkansen (Shinagawa → Kyoto)
⭐ Recommended 2 hrs 15 min ~¥14,000/adult
The fastest option — Nozomi trains run every 10–15 min from Shinagawa. Take the 8:30 AM train, arrive Kyoto by 10:45 AM. Buy Ekiben bento boxes at Shinagawa station for breakfast on the train. Note: JR Pass does NOT cover Nozomi — use Hikari (30 min slower) if using the pass, or pay the Nozomi surcharge (~¥1,000 extra per person).
🚄 Hikari Shinkansen (Tokyo → Kyoto)
2 hrs 45 min JR Pass valid ✓
Covered by JR Pass. Slightly slower than Nozomi but still fast. Runs frequently. Reserve seats at JR ticket office — free with JR Pass.
🚌 Highway Bus (Shinjuku Busta → Kyoto)
~8 hours ~¥4,000/person
Very cheap but not recommended for families with young children — 8 hours is hard on Manraag and wastes an entire sightseeing day. Only worth it on an extreme budget.
⛩️ Kyoto → 🦀 Osaka
Day 6 evening · June 16
LEG 2
🚆 JR Special Rapid (Kyoto → Osaka/Namba)
⭐ Recommended 30 min JR Pass valid ✓
Runs every 15 minutes from Kyoto Station. No reservation needed — just tap IC card or use JR Pass. Arrives Osaka Station in 30 min, then a short subway to Namba. The easiest inter-city trip in Japan.
🚇 Hankyu Limited Express (Kyoto Kawaramachi → Osaka Umeda)
44 min ¥640/person
Scenic private railway — slightly slower but great views. NOT covered by JR Pass. Pay with IC card. Drops off at Umeda (central Osaka) rather than Namba.
🦀 Osaka → 🗼 Tokyo
Day 9 morning · June 19
LEG 3
🚄 Nozomi/Hikari Shinkansen (Shin-Osaka → Shinagawa)
⭐ Recommended 2 hrs 30 min JR Pass (Hikari) ✓
Take the 8:00 AM train from Shin-Osaka. Sit on the RIGHT side (A-seats) going east — Mt. Fuji is visible on the right ~45 min into the journey on clear mornings. This is often the best Fuji view of the whole trip! Arrive Tokyo/Shinagawa by 10:30 AM.
✈️ Fly Osaka (Itami/Kansai) → Tokyo (Haneda)
~1 hr flight + transfers ¥8,000–20,000/person
Door-to-door time (with airport transfers) is similar to the Shinkansen but more expensive and stressful with 8 people and luggage. Not recommended over the train for this group size.
🗼 Tokyo → ✈️ Narita Airport
Day 10 evening · June 20
LEG 4
🚆 Narita Express N'EX (Shinjuku → Narita T1)
⭐ Recommended 80 min JR Pass valid ✓
Direct from Shinjuku to Narita Terminal 1 with no changes. Reserved seats — book all 8 together. Depart Shinjuku ~4:30–5 PM, arrive Narita by 6 PM. Luggage already at Narita hotel via Yamato.
🚌 Airport Limousine Bus (Shinjuku → Narita)
~100 min ¥3,200/adult Good for luggage
Departs from Shinjuku station west exit bus terminal. Easier for carry-on bags as it's step-free. Slightly slower but very comfortable. Kids can rest on the coach.
