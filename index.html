Day 2Friday · June 12
Mt. Fuji 🗻 · Private Van · Pre-Dawn
5 AM departure · Kawaguchiko sunrise · Chureito Pagoda · Oshino Hakkai
Mt. Fuji Day
Private van booked · pick-up at hotel lobby 5:00 AMDriver meets at Shinjuku hotel lobby. ~90 min door-to-door to Lake Kawaguchi. Kids sleep in the van on the way. Book via Klook, Viator or GetYourGuide — search "Mt Fuji private tour 8 passengers Shinjuku". Budget ~¥35,000–55,000 total for the van (split across 2 families). Include Chureito Pagoda, Kawaguchiko, Oshino Hakkai and optional 5th Station in the brief. Book 4–6 weeks ahead.
Why Friday June 12 is the perfect dayWeekday = fewest visitors at all Fuji viewpoints. June mornings are clearest before 9 AM — leaving at 5 AM puts you at the lake by 6:30 AM in the golden window. By noon clouds typically build, so the early start is everything. The 4-year-old can nap in the van both ways.
4:30 AM
Wake up · light breakfast at hotel
Keep it quick — grab onigiri and drinks from the hotel vending machine or the 7-Eleven downstairs. Keep the 4-year-old in warm layers; Fuji area is ~16–18°C at dawn even in June.
Food
5:00 AM
🚐 Private van departs Shinjuku hotel
Driver picks up at lobby. Kids settle in and sleep. Highway is empty at this hour — smooth, fast drive southwest through the dark towards Yamanashi. Pre-dawn Tokyo glittering from the highway is a sight in itself.
Kids sleep in van
6:30 AM
Lake Kawaguchi · Fuji sunrise 🌅
Arrive as the sky shifts from navy to gold. Mt. Fuji — perfectly symmetrical, snow-capped — reflected in the mirror-still lake. Completely empty at this hour. This is the moment that makes the 5 AM wake-up entirely worth it. The 4-year-old waking up to see Fuji for the first time is a memory that lasts forever.
Must-do momentFamily-friendly
7:15 AM
Chureito Pagoda · the iconic Japan photograph 🏯
The 5-storey red pagoda with Fuji perfectly framed behind it — the most famous photograph in Japan. 398 steps up from Shimoyoshida (15 min by van from the lake). The 4-year-old will need carrying up, but every step is worth it. Best light is 7–8 AM. Arrive before any tour buses for the shot of a lifetime.
Most iconic Fuji photo
9:00 AM

10:00 AM
Oshino Hakkai · 8 sacred spring pools 💧
8 crystal-clear pools fed by ancient Mt. Fuji snowmelt — water so clear you can see every stone on the bottom. Traditional thatched village setting. Giant koi fish that the 4-year-old will obsess over. Easy flat walk, completely stroller-friendly. One of the most beautiful spots around Fuji and rarely crowded. 20 min from the lake by van.
Perfect for toddlerMust-do
11:30 AM
Fuji Subaru Line 5th Station (optional) ⛰️
If the sky is still clear, ask the driver to head to the 5th Station (2,305m altitude — halfway up the mountain). Stand on Fuji itself, breathe the thin mountain air, see the volcanic rock and often snow. Spectacular. Takes ~1 hr round trip from Kawaguchiko. Skip if clouds have moved in — not worth the journey if visibility is low.

All legs · Shinkansen · JR Pass recommendation
All four inter-city legs are covered by Shinkansen (bullet train) — fast, comfortable and perfect for families with kids and luggage. A 7-day JR Pass activated on Day 5 (June 15) covers all four Shinkansen legs and pays for itself on the Tokyo–Kyoto leg alone.
💡 JR Pass Recommendation A 7-day JR Pass (activated June 15, valid through June 21) covers: Tokyo→Kyoto, Kyoto→Osaka, Osaka→Tokyo, and the N'EX airport express. Cost: ~¥50,000/adult, ~¥25,000/child. The Tokyo→Kyoto Shinkansen alone costs ~¥14,000 one-way. With 4 adults + 2 teens, the pass pays for itself on Day 1 of activation. Buy online before leaving India at jrpass.com — cannot be bought in Japan.
🗼 Tokyo → ⛩️ Kyoto
Day 5 morning · June 15
LEG 1
🚄 Nozomi Shinkansen (Shinagawa → Kyoto)
⭐ Recommended 2 hrs 15 min ~¥14,000/adult
The fastest option — Nozomi trains run every 10–15 min from Shinagawa. Take the 8:30 AM train, arrive Kyoto by 10:45 AM. Buy Ekiben bento boxes at Shinagawa station for breakfast on the train. Note: JR Pass does NOT cover Nozomi — use Hikari (30 min slower) if using the pass, or pay the Nozomi surcharge (~¥1,000 extra per person).
🚄 Hikari Shinkansen (Tokyo → Kyoto)
2 hrs 45 min JR Pass valid ✓
Covered by JR Pass. Slightly slower than Nozomi but still fast. Runs frequently. Reserve seats at JR ticket office — free with JR Pass.
🚌 Highway Bus (Shinjuku Busta → Kyoto)
~8 hours ~¥4,000/person
Very cheap but not recommended for families with young children — 8 hours is hard on the 4-year-old and wastes an entire sightseeing day. Only worth it on an extreme budget.
⛩️ Kyoto → 🦀 Osaka
Day 6 evening · June 16
LEG 2
🚆 JR Special Rapid (Kyoto → Osaka/Namba)
⭐ Recommended 30 min JR Pass valid ✓
Runs every 15 minutes from Kyoto Station. No reservation needed — just tap IC card or use JR Pass. Arrives Osaka Station in 30 min, then a short subway to Namba. The easiest inter-city trip in Japan.
🚇 Hankyu Limited Express (Kyoto Kawaramachi → Osaka Umeda)
44 min ¥640/person
Scenic private railway — slightly slower but great views. NOT covered by JR Pass. Pay with IC card. Drops off at Umeda (central Osaka) rather than Namba.
🦀 Osaka → 🗼 Tokyo
Day 9 morning · June 19
LEG 3
🚄 Nozomi/Hikari Shinkansen (Shin-Osaka → Shinagawa)
⭐ Recommended 2 hrs 30 min JR Pass (Hikari) ✓
Take the 8:00 AM train from Shin-Osaka. Sit on the RIGHT side (A-seats) going east — Mt. Fuji is visible on the right ~45 min into the journey on clear mornings. This is often the best Fuji view of the whole trip! Arrive Tokyo/Shinagawa by 10:30 AM.
✈️ Fly Osaka (Itami/Kansai) → Tokyo (Haneda)
~1 hr flight + transfers ¥8,000–20,000/person
Door-to-door time (with airport transfers) is similar to the Shinkansen but more expensive and stressful with 8 people and luggage. Not recommended over the train for this group size.
🗼 Tokyo → ✈️ Narita Airport
Day 10 evening · June 20
LEG 4
🚆 Narita Express N'EX (Shinjuku → Narita T1)
⭐ Recommended 80 min JR Pass valid ✓
Direct from Shinjuku to Narita Terminal 1 with no changes. Reserved seats — book all 8 together. Depart Shinjuku ~4:30–5 PM, arrive Narita by 6 PM. Luggage already at Narita hotel via Yamato.
🚌 Airport Limousine Bus (Shinjuku → Narita)
~100 min ¥3,200/adult Good for luggage
Departs from Shinjuku station west exit bus terminal. Easier for carry-on bags as it's step-free. Slightly slower but very comfortable. Kids can rest on the coach.
